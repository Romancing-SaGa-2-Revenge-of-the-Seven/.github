## What Is Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven?

Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven is a modern remake of Square Enix's classic role-playing game. It follows the rulers of the Varennes Empire as they face the Seven Heroes, legendary warriors whose return threatens the world.

A central feature is generational succession. The player leads different emperors and empresses across time, developing the empire while carrying forward knowledge, abilities, and strategic decisions. The story combines imperial management, character recruitment, exploration, and turn-based combat.

The adventure offers a flexible structure in which decisions can affect the order of events, the development of the empire, and the challenges encountered along the way.
